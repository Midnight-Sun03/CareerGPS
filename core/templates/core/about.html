About CareerGPS

CareerGPS helps candidates prepare and navigate applications, and helps approved organisations manage their own hiring work.

For candidates

Organise your profile and professional evidence, discover opportunities, review evidence-based matches, work on applications, prepare CVs and cover letters, and practise interviews.

For employers

Organisations can complete the CareerGPS verification workflow, publish vacancies, manage applicants, review evidence, use opt-in talent discovery, send invitations, and collaborate on hiring stages.

CareerGPS supports decisions with information and explanations. Employers make their own hiring decisions; CareerGPS does not automatically hire or reject candidates.
