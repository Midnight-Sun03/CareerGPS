CV Writing

Build a Standout CV

Learn the key sections every South African graduate CV needs to land interviews.

Step-by-step CV structure

  1. Personal Details: Full name, phone, email, city.
  2. Career Objective: 2-3 lines tailored to the role.
  3. Education: Degree, institution, graduation year.
  4. Work Experience: Use action verbs.
  5. Skills: Communication, Excel, coding, etc.
  6. Achievements: Awards and leadership roles.
Pro Tip

Tailor your career objective to each role. Generic objectives are the #1 reason CVs get rejected by recruiters.

Cover Letters

Write a Cover Letter That Gets Noticed

Learn how to write professional cover letters that stand out from hundreds of applicants.

  • Keep it to one page — concise and targeted.
  • Address the recruiter or hiring manager by name.
  • Open with a hook that shows why you want this role.
  • Mirror keywords from the job description.
Pro Tip

Don't just repeat your CV. Use the cover letter to tell the story behind your experience and show genuine interest in the company.

Interview Tips

Ace Your Next Interview

Practice confidence, communication and the STAR technique to answer any question with clarity.

  • STAR Method: Situation, Task, Action, Result — structure every answer.
  • Research the company before you walk in.
  • Prepare 3–5 questions to ask the interviewer.
  • Body language: Eye contact, firm handshake, upright posture.
Pro Tip

Practice out loud, not just in your head. Record yourself answering common questions and review your delivery and pacing.
