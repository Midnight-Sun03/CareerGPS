Accessibility

CareerGPS offers Dark Mode, High Contrast, Font Size, Keyboard Mode, Reduce Motion, and general Read Aloud where your browser supports speech playback.

These preferences are stored in this browser or device, so you may need to set them again elsewhere. Interview audio playback has its own controls.

We welcome accessibility feedback through Contact. Please describe the page, action, and barrier you encountered.
