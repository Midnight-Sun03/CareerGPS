Employer visibility

You choose whether verified employers can discover your profile. All options start off. Your submitted applications and their document versions remain available to the organizations you applied to.

Contact details are never shown on search cards. Authorized recruiters may reveal them for an active application, even when general contact sharing is off. Hiding a specific organization also prevents contact reveal.
